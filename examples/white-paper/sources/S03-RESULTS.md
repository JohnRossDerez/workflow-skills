# Four skill candidates: bounded writing validation

The four local candidates are Python engineering, repository refactoring, white-paper writing and executive-brief writing. The two engineering packages retain their latest reviewed versions. The writing packages add an executable compact path while retaining legacy full-project compatibility. Final revised evidence and fresh-reader checks passed for all four documents. All three managed projects passed actual release validation; the direct executive brief passed its manual reviews without a configured validator. The candidates are ready for public review with the bounded claims below.

An ordinary internal R&D paper now uses a reader brief, document plan and source/claim/passage ledger. ## Interpretation and limits

The observed initial errors and later source correction were repairable without a full writing graph in these compact cases. Together with genuine review and retained freshness behavior, that supports keeping compact as a usable default for this bounded internal R&D use. The full representation remains available for needs this study did not test. Direct drafting is still appropriate for proportional short briefs.

One execution per audience/condition cannot establish average effectiveness, statistical superiority, general writing quality or the isolated effect of one instruction. Both prior engineering conditions passed their frozen task suites; later discovery demonstrates the oracle's incomplete live-policy coverage rather than proving which instruction package is best. Author release recommendations in example documents are evidence-grounded judgments, not authoritative product decisions for this collection.

Cooperative isolation is not adversarial filesystem security. Native thread-limit rejections were recorded; rejected roles did not participate. Complete worker-inclusive token usage, cache savings and serving-backend identity are unavailable, so there is no cost or efficiency claim. Markdown-only cases do not establish rendered PDF/Word quality. No fifth skill, installation or external publication is included.
