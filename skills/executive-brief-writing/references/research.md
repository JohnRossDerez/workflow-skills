# Research and reference quality

For compact projects, record consequential searches, exclusions and stopping decisions in the brief or a short research note only when research warrants it. The evidence registers and truth standard below remain shared.

## Search and appraisal

Start from the selected mode's questions. Define material claims, what would support or weaken them, and the evidence needed. Search broadly enough to identify relevant mechanisms, then narrow to decisive sources. Use current retrieval for facts that may have changed. Inspect source contents, not only search snippets.

Record query, date, searched collection, selected sources, consequential exclusions, and stopping decisions in `research/search-log.md`. A targeted brief needs a compact log; extended research needs explicit inclusion criteria. Do not manufacture a comprehensive-search claim from a selective commission.

Assess each source for directness, measurement validity, scope, date, independence, and conflicts of interest. Prefer original records, datasets, filings, standards, and technical documentation when they answer the actual question. A prestigious publisher does not compensate for measuring the wrong concept. Vendor documentation can establish configuration while its performance or commercial statements remain attributed assertions.

Preserve the original source family: multiple articles repeating one estimate are not independent corroboration. Record original reports and transformations behind reused internal conclusions. A prior report's conclusion carries its assumptions forward.

## Claims and evidence links

Use atomic material claims. Separate observation, company claim, derived value, assumption, projection, and interpretation. The claim register records support status, publication authorization where applicable, qualification, and the actual report excerpt. Evidence links record the exact passage/page/table/cell, a short excerpt or precise extract, relation (direct/partial/context/contradicts), and reviewer assessment.

Verification means a person or agent actually inspected the passage and assessed support. A successful fetch, a citation count, or approval to quote a company statement is not verification of its underlying truth. Distinguish correctly reporting an estimate from validating its predictive accuracy. Context-only citations cannot establish a factual premise.

For a material contradiction, explain its disposition and narrow the claim if necessary. For confidential company claims, preserve an authorized internal source record and make attribution explicit in the report. Do not expose confidential extracts in external output.

## Quantitative claims

Preserve inputs, units, geography, time basis, currency basis, transformations, inclusion/exclusion rules, formulas, code, and output tables. Use a calculation record with paths to reproducible inputs and outputs. Scripts never execute calculation expressions from records automatically; inspect and run the authorized analysis code separately.

Put consequential repeated values in `evidence/numbers.json` and use `{{number:ID}}` in the manuscript. Record raw value, display value, decimal places, units, time basis, and calculation/source path. Separate percent from fraction; distinguish monthly from annual, stock from flow, asking price from transaction, accounting life from resale life, and capacity from deployed supply.

A scalar rounding check does not prove compounding consistency. For rates, preserve precision sufficient to reproduce published endpoints, or omit an approximate rate that would imply a materially different path. Independently reconstruct the decisive endpoints and their sensitivities.

Document proxy mechanism, target mismatch, expected bias, and sensitivity. Scenarios require labeled assumptions and a mechanism; they do not become forecasts because they have precise curves. Source prestige, data volume, and standards-based terminology do not establish model calibration or accuracy.

## References and stopping

Archive material retrieved evidence locally when permitted. If retention is unavailable, record the reason and retain a precise locator and retrieval date. Do not invent publication dates for undated pages. Use a stable URL or DOI where available; `source_id` is also the manuscript citation key, avoiding a separate unmaintained mapping.

Stop when the decisive questions are answered to the brief's standard and material countertests are resolved. If further research is unlikely to help or the budget is exhausted, narrow the answer or disclose the gap. A source count is not a quality target.
